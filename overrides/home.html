Devices

Custom firmware for TrimUI handhelds

It runs from the SD card and replaces the stock interface. Update the stock firmware to the version shown before installing.

  • BrickStock firmware 1.1.1
  • Brick HammerStock firmware 1.1.1
  • Brick ProStock firmware 1.1.1 or 1.1.2
  • Smart Pro SStock firmware 1.0.1 or 1.0.2. The fastest for PSP, Dreamcast and N64.
  • Smart ProStock firmware 1.1.1. Should work, but untested.

An app for Android 8.0 and later

Any 64-bit ARM device. The layout follows the screen you play on.

  • PhonesA pad skin under the game in portrait, beside it in landscape.
  • TabletsSkin layouts of their own for the bigger screen.
  • FoldablesHalf-folded, the game sits above the hinge and the pad below.
  • Android handheldsBuilt-in buttons just work. A second screen is used too.
  • ControllersAny Android controller, Bluetooth or USB. Up to four at once.
  • External displaysPlug in a screen and the game moves to it.

Get it

On the SD card in five steps

  1. Update the stock firmwareTo at least the version listed for your device above.
  2. Format a microSD cardexFAT, on a card from a reputable brand.
  3. Copy the releaseDownload the zip for your exact model, extract it and copy everything to the card. Leave MinUI.zip zipped.
  4. Add your gamesInto each system's folder under Roms, and BIOS files under Bios.
  5. Power onThe installer runs by itself on first boot. Later updates install over Wi-Fi.

Playing in four steps

  1. Download the APKFrom the releases page, on the phone itself.
  2. Install itAndroid asks to allow installs from your browser the first time. Allow it, then Install.

FAQ

Can I move my SD card between devices?

No. A card is built for one model. Device Sync carries saves, save states and, if you like, ROMs from one device to another.

How do I update?

From Settings → About over Wi-Fi, or by copying the new MinUI.zip to the card. Emulators and tools update with it.

Do I need to supply BIOS files?

For some systems, yes. They are copyrighted and not included. The emulator pages list which file goes where.

Is there a RetroAchievements hardcore mode?

No, by design, to keep your account safe. Softcore unlocks work fully, offline too.

A game won't launch. Where do I start?

Check for a missing BIOS first, then the romset for arcade games, then the emulator's log on the SD card.

All handheld questions

Do I need to download emulators or cores?

No. Every emulator is bundled in the app.

Can I use the same games folder as my handheld?

Yes. A copy of your handheld's folders, BIOS files included, works as the app's home folder.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Two to four people play one game, each on their own controller. Press a button on each controller to join.

Does it work on foldables?

Yes. Half-folded, the game moves above the fold and the controls go below it, automatically.

Can it be my home screen?

Yes. Turn on Use as home screen in Tools → Settings → System.

Is there an iOS version?

Not yet. NX Redux runs on Android only for now.
