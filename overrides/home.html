How it works

Play in minutes, without configuring emulators

No frontend wiring, no RetroArch cores to hunt down, and no separate emulator apps. Everything is built in and ready out of the box.

  1. 1 Download one release Download one app All 41 systems and tools ship together in one zip for your TrimUI. All 39 systems - from NES to PS2, GameCube, Wii, Wii U and 3DS - are inside one APK.
  2. 2 Add your ROMs Point to your ROMs folder Copy the release to a microSD card and drop games into Roms/. Pick your existing folder - scanned in place without moving or renaming files.
  3. 3 Start playing Controls, auto-save on quit, netplay and achievements already work. Per-console pad skins, controllers, shaders and auto-save already work.

Get it

On the SD card in four steps

Brick · Requires stock firmware 1.1.1

Download {{ config.extra.handheld_version }} on GitHub Full install guide

Free & open source · Runs from microSD · Later updates install over Wi-Fi

  1. Update the stock firmwareTo at least the version listed for your model above.
  2. Format a microSD card as exFATOn a card from a reputable brand.
  3. Copy the releaseDownload the zip for your exact model, extract it and copy everything to the card. Leave MinUI.zip zipped.
  4. Add your games and power onDrop ROMs under Roms/ and BIOS files under Bios/. First boot installs automatically.

{# ── FAQ ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

FAQ

Do I need to install or configure emulators myself?

No. All 41 systems ship pre-installed and configured on the SD card. Add your ROMs and play.

Can I move my SD card between devices?

No. A card is built for one model. Device Sync carries saves, save states and, if you like, ROMs from one device to another.

How do I update?

From Settings → About over Wi-Fi, or by copying the new MinUI.zip to the card. Emulators and tools update with it.

Do I need to supply BIOS files?

For some systems, yes. They are copyrighted and not included. The emulator pages list which file goes where.

Is there a RetroAchievements hardcore mode?

No, by design, to keep your account safe. Softcore unlocks work fully, offline too.

A game won't launch. Where do I start?

Check for a missing BIOS first, then the romset for arcade games, then the emulator's log on the SD card.

All handheld questions

Do I need RetroArch or other emulator apps?

No. Supported emulators are built straight into NX Redux - download the app, point it to your ROMs folder and play.

Will it move or rename my existing ROMs?

No. Extra ROM folders are scanned in place - nothing is moved or rewritten.

Can I use the same games folder as my handheld?

Yes. A copy of your handheld's folders, BIOS files included, works as the app's home folder.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Two to four people play one game, each on their own controller. Press a button on each controller to join.

Does it work on foldables?

Yes. Half-folded, the game moves above the fold and the controls go below it, automatically.

Can it be my home screen?

Yes. Turn on Use as home screen in Tools → Settings → System.

Is there an iOS version?

Not yet. NX Redux runs on Android only for now.
